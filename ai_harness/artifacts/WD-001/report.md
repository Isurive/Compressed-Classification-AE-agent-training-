# WD-001: can tuned weight decay replace the reconstruction loss in a wide head?

Author: `claude-orient-20261122-a` (coordinator acting as engineer). Date: 2026-11-27. User approval: "ok lets do the weight decay" (2026-11-27). Design pre-registered in [task.json](task.json) before any run. Phase-A selection locked in task WD-001S before any ImageNetV2 run, and reviewed in REVIEW-013 (accepted, cross-reviewed).

## 1. Answer

The answer depends on the head:

- **Dense head (M 2048, k 2048).** Tuned weight decay comes close to the reconstruction (feature-MSE) loss but not all the way. The CE-only head with tuned weight decay is **0.29 pp below** the MSE-trained head (W1: 95% CI [−0.41, −0.17], 0/7 seeds positive, Holm-detected). This gap is **below both the 1.0 pp working tolerance and 0.5 pp**.
- **Sparse head (M 2048, k 512).** Tuned weight decay does **not** replace the reconstruction loss. The CE-only head with tuned weight decay is **1.36 pp below** the MSE-trained head (W2: [−1.52, −1.20], 0/7, Holm-detected, meaningful at 1.0 pp). This is the pre-declared outcome "the reconstruction loss gives a benefit tuned weight decay does not". The caveat in §4 applies: this head's selected setting sits at the edge of the grid.
- **Neither weight-decay head beats the linear head.** dense-CE+wd − linear is −0.35 pp [−0.57, −0.14] (Holm-detected; not meaningful at 1.0 or 0.5 pp). sparse-CE+wd − linear is −1.34 pp [−1.63, −1.05] (Holm-detected; meaningful at 1.0 pp).

W1's outcome is not one of the two readings pre-declared in task.json. Those were "not detected or positive" and "negative and meaningful", and W1 is **detected, negative and below tolerance**. The wording above ("close, but not all the way; gap below tolerance") is therefore a post-hoc description of that case, not a pre-declared reading.

All four tests are confirmatory: pre-registered, Holm-corrected over the 4 tests, paired by seed 45–51 (n = 7, df 6, t = 2.447). Results are pooled over source, L080 and L090 as the per-seed mean of the per-level differences. The metric is ImageNetV2 matched-frequency top-1 on source (uncompressed) evaluation. The comparison runs (dense-MSE, sparse-MSE at lr 1e-4; linear at lr 3e-4; all 20 epochs) are RETEST-001's runs on the same seeds, levels and splits.

## 4. Caveats on reading W1–W4

- **sparse-CE may be under-trained.** It was selected at the grid's smallest lr (3e-5) with the budget binding. In phase B, 16/21 runs picked epoch 19 or 20 (median 19), compared with median 11 and 0/21 for dense-CE. A longer budget or an lr between 3e-5 and 1e-4 might narrow W2 and W4. That would be a new, post-hoc experiment. The pre-registered comparison holds all heads to the same 20-epoch budget, as RETEST-001 did, and within that budget the result stands.
- **dense-CE's setting came from an exact tie.** The tied alternative (lr 3e-5) was not run on the test set, and should not be, since that would be test-set selection.
- **Tuning effort differs, in favour of the CE heads.** The CE heads chose among 24 candidates (3 lr × 4 wd × 2 budgets). The MSE heads' lr 1e-4 came from LR-002's learning-rate check (lr only, no weight-decay search; for sparse M2048 it was chosen post-hoc, COORD-004 O-2). The linear head's weight decay was set at lr 1e-3 and not re-tuned (COORD-004 D). If anything this favours the CE heads, so it does not explain why they fall short.
- **Weight-decay form.** Coupled L2 through Adam's `weight_decay` (the form the linear head uses), not decoupled AdamW. It applied to the encoder and classifier weights and biases only, not to the learned centre or the training-only decoder atoms. AdamW, or decay on all parameters, could behave differently.
- **Scope.** One backbone (frozen ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 features), M = 2048 only, k = 512 for the sparse head (set at lr 1e-3, not re-tuned), CE weights as ABL-001 'abl-none' (reconstruction weight 1e-6, dependency 0, coherence 0).
- **Test-set reuse.** ImageNetV2 has been scored many times in this project. The comparison runs' test results were already known when WD-001 was designed. No WD-001 setting was chosen using test data.
